# Financial Performance Dashboard (2018–2020) | Tableau

An interactive Tableau dashboard and PDF report built from a **27,909-line general ledger** covering 7 countries.

## What it shows
- Profit & loss, balance sheet and net margin by country
- Year and region filters
- A check that **Assets = Liabilities + Equity** in every year

## Key findings
- Net revenue grew **119%** from 2018 to 2020
- Net margin fell from **22.9% to 16.5%** in 2020
- Commissions climbed to **10.5%** of revenue
- Australia ran at a **−19.2%** net margin
